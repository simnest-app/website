4. Things to know

  1. Emergency calls: The Service can't be used to call emergency numbers, whether mainland China's 110, 119, 120 or 122 or other countries' and regions' (such as 112, 911, 999 or 000). The gateway phone is at home, so a call through it would give the emergency centre the gateway's location and number and could delay help. In an emergency, call from a phone near you. Both apps block these numbers and show your region's emergency numbers.
  2. Availability: The Service depends on the gateway phone's battery, network and system settings (such as battery saving and background limits), on the carrier, on your iPhone's network and on our servers. If any of them fails, calls may not come through and messages may be delayed or lost. Don't rely on the Service alone for important calls and messages.
  3. Message results: "Sent" means the carrier accepted the message, not that it was delivered. Resending one whose result is unknown may deliver it twice.
  4. Two SIMs: On a dual-SIM, single-active phone, a call on one SIM makes the other SIM's incoming calls busy.
  5. Default apps: The gateway phone needs SimNest Gateway as its default SMS and phone app. The gateway phone itself has no full calling interface.
  6. Family access: What family can do follows the rights the owner sets, which the owner can change or revoke at any time. If the gateway can't confirm the latest rights for 5 minutes, calls on iPhones are ended and messages pause until it confirms.
  7. Verification codes: With automatic copying on, codes you receive are put on the iPhone clipboard, which other apps may read.
  8. Test versions: Versions distributed through TestFlight and the like may be unstable, and features may change or stop.
